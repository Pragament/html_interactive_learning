📋 Observation Table
Food Item Paper Appearance after Drying Translucent Oily Spot? Contains Fat?
Peanuts Oily translucent patch YES YES (Rich in Fat)
Dry Coconut Oily translucent patch YES YES (Rich in Fat)
Butter Large greasy translucent patch YES YES (High Fat)
Rice Grains Dry, clean paper (no spot) NO NO (Carbohydrate)
