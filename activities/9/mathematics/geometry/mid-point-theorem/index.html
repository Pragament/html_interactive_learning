Mid-point Theorem

Class IX · Mathematics · Triangles

The Mid-point Theorem: If a line segment joins the mid-points of two sides of a triangle, then it is parallel to the third side, and its length is half the length of the third side.

  1. Click on two sides of the triangle below to select them.
  2. Click "Mark Midpoints" to place points M and N at the exact middle of each selected side.
  3. Click "Draw Segment MN" to join the two mid-points.
  4. Click "Compare with Third Side" to see that MN is parallel to the remaining side.
