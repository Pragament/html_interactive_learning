What to notice

  • Corresponding angles are equal — same corner, at each intersection.
  • Alternate interior angles are equal — between the parallel lines, on opposite sides of the transversal.
  • Vertically opposite angles are equal — opposite corners at the same intersection.
  • Co-interior angles are supplementary (they add up to 180°) — between the parallel lines, on the same side of the transversal.

Teacher prompt

Project the diagram for the whole class. Before moving the slider, ask students to predict which angles will stay equal and which will change.

  • Set a steep or flat transversal and ask: "Which two angles are still equal, no matter the angle?"
  • Use Explore Mode for think-pair-share: one student picks two angles, the class predicts the relationship before revealing it.
  • Switch to Challenge Mode for a quick formative check — the running score works well as an exit-ticket target.
  • Press Reset between groups to return to the default 60° transversal.
