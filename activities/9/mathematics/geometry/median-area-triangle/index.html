Teacher Guide & Classroom Pedagogy

Learning Objective: Students discover and prove that a median of a triangle divides it into two triangles of equal area.

Classroom Prompt: Ask students: "Why do △ABM and △AMC have equal areas even when the triangle is scalene or obtuse?" (Guide them to observe that both triangles share the exact same base length $BM = MC$ and the exact same perpendicular height from vertex $A$).
