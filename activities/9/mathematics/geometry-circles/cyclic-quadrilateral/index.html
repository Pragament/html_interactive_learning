Cyclic Quadrilateral

Interactive Mathematics Activity • Class IX–X

Learning Objective

Theorem: In a cyclic quadrilateral, the sum of each pair of opposite angles is 180°.

A quadrilateral is called a cyclic quadrilateral when all four of its vertices lie on the same circle.

What Do You Observe?

Step 1: All four vertices A, B, C and D lie on the same circle.
Step 2: Therefore, ABCD is a cyclic quadrilateral.
Step 3: Move any vertex around the circle.
Step 4: The individual angles change.
Step 5: The opposite angles always add up to 180°.
