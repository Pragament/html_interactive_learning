Why are opposite angles supplementary?

Drag any vertex around the circle and watch what happens.

Key idea: an inscribed angle is half the arc it faces. ∠A faces the amber arc, ∠C faces the pink arc. Those two arcs together make the whole circle (360°), so ∠A + ∠C = 360°/2 = 180°.

Drag A, B, C or D — the sum never changes. You can also set ∠A above.
