Angle in a Semi-circle

Interactive Geometry Activity • Class IX–X

Theorem

The angle subtended by a diameter at any point on the semicircle is always 90°.

Measure ∠ACB

90°

Point C: Drag the red point around the semicircle.

The triangle changes shape, but the angle at C remains a right angle.

What do you observe?

A and B are the endpoints of the diameter. C can be any point on the semicircle.

When C moves, the lengths AC and BC change. However, the angle ACB remains 90°.

Therefore, ∠ACB = 90°.
