Parallel Lines and a Transversal

Explore corresponding, alternate and interior angles

Learning Activity

Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. Move the transversal angle using the slider and observe how the eight angles change.

  • Corresponding angles are equal.
  • Alternate interior angles are equal.
  • Interior angles on the same side of the transversal add to 180°.
