Explore quadratic expressions using an interactive algebra-tile model.

5. Verify by Multiplication

(x + 2)(x + 3) = x² + 3x + 2x + 6 = x² + 5x + 6

This shows that the factors multiply back to the original quadratic polynomial.

Teacher Mode

Use the following questions while demonstrating the activity.

  • What does the x² tile represent?
  • Why are there b x-tiles and c unit tiles?
  • Which pair of numbers has a product equal to c?
  • How does the sum of the factor pair relate to b?
  • What happens when the constant term is negative?
  • Can every quadratic polynomial be factorised using integer factors?

Suggested Classroom Activity

  1. Set the polynomial to x² + 5x + 6.
  2. Ask students to identify the x², x and unit tiles.
  3. Ask students to find two numbers whose product is 6.
  4. Ask which pair adds to 5.
  5. Use the tiles to form a rectangle.
  6. Connect the rectangle dimensions with (x+2)(x+3).
  7. Verify the result by expanding the factors.

Learning Check

Ask students to explain factorisation in their own words instead of only giving the final factors.
