A five-stage interactive activity for NCERT Class X Similar Triangles. Students
change the Sun angle to compare proportional heights and shadows, learn and
apply similarity criteria, solve a guided shadow problem, take a quick challenge,
and complete two CBSE-style case studies adapted from MathsX.pdf: finding the
heights of buildings and a tower using shadows, and measuring a distance across
a pond using right triangles.
