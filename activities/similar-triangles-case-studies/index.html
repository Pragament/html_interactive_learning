What makes triangles similar?

Two triangles are similar when they have the same shape, even if one is larger. Their corresponding angles match, and every corresponding side is enlarged or reduced by one common scale factor.

1Match the anglesThe vertical object and level ground make a 90° angle in both triangles.
2Use the same Sun rayParallel sunlight gives both triangles the same angle of elevation.
3Compare corresponding sidesHeight matches height; shadow matches shadow. Their ratios must be equal.

height₁ / shadow₁ = height₂ / shadow₂

What should you discover?

Invariant ratioAt one Sun angle, height ÷ shadow stays equal for every vertical object.
Scale factorIf all corresponding lengths multiply by k, the triangles remain similar.
Changing angleA new Sun angle changes the ratio, but both objects still share that new ratio.
Worked reading: At 45°, a 10 m house has a 10 m shadow. A 40 m tower is 4 times taller, so its matching shadow is 4 × 10 = 40 m.
Real survey method: Measure a known object and its shadow, then measure the unknown object's shadow at the same time. Use H₁/S₁ = H₂/S₂ to find the unknown height without climbing it.

When are two triangles similar?

Similar triangles have the same shape, though their sizes may differ. Corresponding angles are equal and corresponding sides are proportional.

AB/DE = BC/EF = AC/DF
  • AA: two corresponding angles are equal.
  • SSS: all three pairs of corresponding sides are proportional.
  • SAS: two pairs of sides are proportional and the included angles are equal.

Why the shadow method works

1. The person/building and the ground form a right angle.
2. Sun rays are parallel, so the angle of elevation is the same.
3. The triangles are similar by AA.
4. Therefore height₁/shadow₁ = height₂/shadow₂.

Important: compare measurements taken at the same time. If the Sun angle changes, the ratio changes.

Pond survey idea

A surveyor measures accessible land segments and constructs right triangles. Pythagoras finds a diagonal; a straight-line subtraction or a similarity proportion then reveals the inaccessible length without entering the pond.
