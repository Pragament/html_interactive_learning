Real paint note: Paint mixing is subtractive, so real paints do not always produce exactly the same shade shown here. The final color depends on the pigments, amount of each paint, opacity and brand. This activity assumes roughly equal amounts of paint.
For teachers Ask students to predict the result before revealing it. After mixing, discuss why paint mixing is different from mixing colored light. The common primary paint colors red, yellow and blue can produce many secondary and intermediate colors.
