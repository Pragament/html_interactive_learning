🧊 Ice Melting & Water Evaporation

Interactive Science Experiment — Physical and Chemical Changes

🔬 About the Experiment

In this virtual experiment, you can control the amount of heat given to ice and water. Observe how ice melts into water and how water changes into water vapour when heated.


At high heating, water vapour rises and touches the cooler inverted watch glass, where it condenses into water droplets.

🎯 Learning Objectives

🧊 Understand melting of ice.
💧 Understand evaporation of water.
☁️ Observe formation of water vapour.
💦 Observe condensation.

🔄 Reversibility

The changes in this experiment are physical changes because the substance remains water and can change between different physical states.

⚖️ Physical vs Chemical Change

Physical Change Chemical Change
No new substance is formed A new substance is formed
Often reversible Often difficult to reverse
Only physical state/form changes Chemical composition changes
Example: melting ice Example: rusting iron

⭐ Key Takeaways

🧊 Heating ice causes it to melt.
💧 Water changes into vapour during evaporation.
☁️ Water vapour can condense back into liquid water.
🔄 These are physical changes.
🧪 No new substance is produced.
