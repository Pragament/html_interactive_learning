👩‍🏫 Teacher Guide

Learning objective: Students observe that a current-carrying wire produces a magnetic effect, detected here by a compass needle.

Suggested explanation: A compass contains a small magnetic needle. When current flows through the nearby wire, the magnetic effect of the current interacts with the needle and makes it turn.

Demonstration sequence:

  1. Start with the switch open and ask students to observe the needle.
  2. Close the switch and ask: “What changed?”
  3. Open the switch again to compare the two situations.
  4. Reverse the cell connections and close the switch. Ask students to observe the changed direction of deflection.

💬 Ask the class

  • Why does the compass move when the switch is closed?
  • What happens when the switch is opened?
  • What happens when the cell direction is reversed?
  • What does the compass help us detect?
Teacher tip: Keep the explanation qualitative. At this level, focus on the observation: current in the wire produces a magnetic effect.
