Quick Introduction

Let's understand the three important ideas before entering the lab.

🍋

Acids

Acids are substances that show acidic properties. Examples in this activity include lemon juice and vinegar.

🧼

Bases

Bases show basic properties. Soap solution is an example of a basic substance.

🎨

Indicators

Indicators are substances that show a characteristic colour change in acidic or basic solutions.

💡 Key Takeaways

Remember these important ideas.

🔵 Blue LitmusTurns red in an acidic solution and remains blue in a basic solution.
🟡 TurmericShows a reddish-brown colour in a basic solution.
🌹 China RoseShows pink/magenta in acids and green in bases.
