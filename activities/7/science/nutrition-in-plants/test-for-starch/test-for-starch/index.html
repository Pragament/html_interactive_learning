💡 What did you discover?

Iodine solution turns blue-black when starch is present. A leaf exposed to light can make food by photosynthesis, and some of that food may be stored as starch.

A leaf kept in darkness may show less starch if stored starch was used up beforehand. In a variegated leaf, green regions usually contain chlorophyll and may form starch when exposed to light, while white regions generally do not.

This simulator demonstrates typical results. Actual outcomes depend on the plant and the experiment conditions. In a classroom experiment, a teacher may destarch the plant and safely decolourize the leaf before adding iodine.
