💡 Remember

Green plants make food through photosynthesis using light energy, water, and carbon dioxide. Chlorophyll captures light energy. Roots absorb water and mineral nutrients from soil, while carbon dioxide enters mainly through tiny openings in leaves called stomata.

Plants also need suitable conditions to grow. Too little or too much water, insufficient light, or limited carbon dioxide can restrict growth. Minerals are essential for healthy development but are not a substitute for the raw materials of photosynthesis.
