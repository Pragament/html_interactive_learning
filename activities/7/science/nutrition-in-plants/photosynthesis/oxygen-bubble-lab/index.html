💡 Conclusion

In aquatic plants, oxygen released during photosynthesis can sometimes be observed as bubbles. When light is very low, the rate of photosynthesis is often low. Increasing light can increase the rate, but only while other needs are sufficiently met. If carbon dioxide is limited or the temperature is unsuitable, the rate may remain low.
