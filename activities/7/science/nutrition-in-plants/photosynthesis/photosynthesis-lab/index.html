🔬 Mission briefing

Plants use light energy to make food from water and carbon dioxide. Chlorophyll, the green pigment in leaves, helps capture light energy. Oxygen is released during this process.

Remember: Light, water and carbon dioxide are important inputs. In this simplified model, the scarcest input limits the photosynthesis rate.

This is a learning simulation, not a precise biological calculator. Real plants are affected by many other factors too.
