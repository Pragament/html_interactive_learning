QUICK START

  1. Ready: fit 3 AAA batteries underneath. Match + / −; flat − ends touch springs. Close the cover. Put the sheet on a flat floor.
  2. On: slide the bottom switch to the middle. Press ACTION (✱) + REVERSE (▼) together to switch between Grid and Draw modes.
  3. First move: face along a line. Press CLEAR (X) → FORWARD (▲) → green GO. Watch how far Wizbot moves.
  4. Plan: CLEAR, then ▲ forward, ▼ back, ◀ / ▶ turn. Tap again for more moves. GO runs your plan.

Grid Mode: straight steps and 90° turns.
Draw Mode (purple): shorter steps, angled turns and curves. ▲ + ◀ / ▶ together: 45°; ▼ + ◀ / ▶: 30°. Hold ◀ / ▶ for 1 second (one chime): curve.

Try again: CLEAR before a new plan. Follow the same pattern at crossings. Count gaps using the edge numbers; a dot gap may differ from a robot step.

More Wizbot adventures

From the Wizbot Maxx student manual. Try these after learning the buttons.

Move in a circle

Use purple Draw Mode. Press CLEAR. Hold RIGHT for 1 second until a short chime; repeat this four times, then press GO. The robot’s circle may differ from the printed circle.

Try a wider curve

In Draw Mode, hold LEFT or RIGHT for 3 seconds until a double chime to queue a wide arc. Press GO to run it.

Code with blocks

Switch power to Picto-Coding (blue light). Enable Bluetooth on your tablet or computer. Open Junior Blocks, select Board → Wizbot Maxx, then choose your robot’s Bluetooth ID. A chime confirms the connection. Build a sequence of move and turn blocks, then run it.

If moves become wobbly

Check that the surface is flat and the wheels move freely. If the low-battery red light appears, replace all three AAA batteries together with fresh batteries of the same brand.
