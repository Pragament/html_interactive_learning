What are we learning?

The Basic Proportionality Theorem states that if a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle and intersects the other two sides, then it divides those two sides in the same ratio.

Basic Proportionality Theorem
If DE ∥ BC, then AD / DB = AE / EC
1

Draw the triangle

Consider triangle ABC and choose points D and E on AB and AC respectively.

2

Draw the parallel

Draw DE parallel to BC. The two sides are now divided proportionally.

3

Compare the ratios

Calculate AD/DB and AE/EC. Both ratios should be equal.

Think about it: What happens to AD/DB and AE/EC when you move the parallel line closer to B and C? Try different positions and check whether the two ratios remain equal.
