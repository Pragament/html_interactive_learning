📚 What will you learn?

Observe & Group

Materials around us have different properties. We can observe these properties and group similar materials together.

Everyday Examples

For example, some materials are hard, some are soft. Some dissolve in water, while others do not. Some let light pass through (transparent), while others block light (opaque).
