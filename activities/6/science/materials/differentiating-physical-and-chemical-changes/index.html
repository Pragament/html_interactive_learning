🔬 Physical and Chemical Changes

Class VI | Science | Topic: Materials

Discover how materials change around us!

🎯 Learning Objective

To understand the difference between physical and chemical changes by observing the folding, tearing, and burning of paper.

📘 What Are Physical and Chemical Changes?

Physical Change

A change in the shape, size, or state of a substance without forming a new substance.

Physical

Chemical Change

A change in which one or more new substances are formed with different properties.

Chemical

Some physical changes are reversible, while others are irreversible. Chemical changes usually form new substances.

💡 Conclusion

Folding and tearing paper are physical changes because no new substance is formed. Folding is usually reversible, while tearing is irreversible.

Burning paper is a chemical change because new substances, such as ash and gases, are formed.

⚠️ Safety Note

Never burn paper on your own.

Burning is shown as a virtual simulation in this activity. Any real burning experiment must only be performed by a teacher in a properly supervised laboratory with appropriate safety equipment.
