Compare Water Absorption Capacity of Fibres

Class VI Science · Topic: Materials

🎯 Objective

To compare how much water cotton, wool, and nylon fabrics absorb by measuring their dry and wet masses.

🧰 Materials Required

Cotton fabric Wool fabric Nylon fabric Water Beakers or bowls Balance / weighing scale Tongs or forceps Paper towels

📝 Procedure

  1. Cut equal-sized pieces of cotton, wool, and nylon fabric.
  2. Make sure each piece is dry, then measure and record its dry mass.
  3. Immerse each fabric piece in water for the same amount of time.
  4. Remove each piece and allow excess water to drip off for the same duration.
  5. Measure and record the wet mass of each fabric piece.
  6. Calculate the water absorbed per gram of dry fabric.
  7. Compare the results to identify which fabric absorbed more water.
Fair test: Use equal-sized fabric pieces, the same soaking time, and the same drip-drying time for all samples.

The fabric with the greatest calculated water absorbed per gram has the highest water absorption capacity in this experiment. Actual results may vary depending on the fabric, weave, thickness, and experimental conditions.
