🧪 Identification of Salts

Class VI | Science | Topic: Materials

Explore acidic, basic and neutral solutions!

🎯 Learning Objective

To identify acidic, basic and neutral salt solutions using red and blue litmus paper.

📘 What is Litmus Paper?

Litmus paper is an indicator used to test whether a solution is acidic or basic.

  • Red litmus: Turns blue in a basic solution.
  • Blue litmus: Turns red in an acidic solution.
  • Neutral solution: Neither paper changes color.

Formula: NaCl

💡 Conclusion

Litmus paper helps us identify the nature of a solution. Ferric chloride solution is acidic, sodium acetate solution is basic, and sodium chloride solution is neutral.

By observing the color changes of red and blue litmus paper, we can classify these solutions.
