💡 Class 6 Key Concepts & Ray Diagrams
Understand how light rays interact with different everyday materials.
🪟
1. Transparent Materials
Materials that allow almost all light to pass straight through them. We can see objects clearly through transparent materials.
📜
2. Translucent Materials
Materials that allow only a small part of light to pass through. Objects behind them look hazy or blurry.
🪵
3. Opaque Materials
Materials that completely block light. Light cannot pass through them at all, which creates a dark SHADOW behind!
