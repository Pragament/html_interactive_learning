Test for Carbohydrates (Starch)

Dilute Iodine Test on Bread, Potato, and Chickpea seeds

🧪
Reagent: Dilute Iodine Solution (Amber / Brownish-Yellow)
Turns blue-black only in the presence of starch.

Food Sample Colour Observed Starch Present? Primary Nutrient
Bread Slice No drops added — Carbohydrates (Starch)
Potato Slice No drops added — Carbohydrates (Starch)
Soaked Chickpeas No drops added — Proteins
💡 Scientific Explanation:
When iodine solution interacts with starch (present in bread and potato), it slips into the coiled amylose structure to form an intense blue-black complex.
In soaked chickpeas, protein is the primary nutrient; hence the iodine remains its natural yellowish-brown colour with no blue-black reaction.
