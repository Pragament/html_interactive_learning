Germination of Seeds

Soak gram/moong seeds, keep them moist on a wet cloth and observe daily. Use the interactive timeline below to record observations and discuss with your group.

Activity steps
💧
Day 0 — Soak
Soak seeds in water for 24 hours, then drain.
🧽
Day 1 — Wrap
Place seeds on wet cotton and cover; keep moist.
🌱
Day 2 — Radicle appears
A white root (radicle) emerges — measure length and note color.
🌿
Day 3 — Shoot growth
Shoot and first leaves appear; record changes in height.
Teacher note:

Use a ruler to measure root length and record daily. Discuss why moisture and oxygen are needed for germination. Emphasize safety: this is a classroom demonstration; avoid tasting or using chemicals.
