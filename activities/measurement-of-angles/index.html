Types of Angles

An angle is formed when two rays meet at a common endpoint called the vertex. Angles are classified according to their measurement.

Acute 0° < θ < 90°
Right θ = 90°
Obtuse 90° < θ < 180°
Straight θ = 180°
Reflex 180° < θ < 360°
One complete revolution = 360°
